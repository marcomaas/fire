      What this map shows

      The red area is the mapped burnt area as assessed from satellite imagery by the Copernicus Emergency Management Service. The sequence moves from one acquisition to the next, showing how the fire expanded over the days. The white outline places a city at the same location for comparison, preserving its true area so the order of magnitude becomes directly legible.

      How to read the figures

      Timestamp
      Acquisition time of the underlying satellite scene, not the time of publication.
      Movement between two acquisitions
      Linearly interpolated. This is a presentational aid: what happened between two overpasses cannot be known from this data.
      Area figure
      Sum of all mapped patches for that state. Where one contiguous area dominates, its outline is morphed; where the fire is scattered across many patches, each state is faded in as a whole instead.
      City comparison
      The outline is moved to the fire location while keeping its actual area. Because a degree of longitude is shorter at higher latitudes, the east-west extent is corrected accordingly.
